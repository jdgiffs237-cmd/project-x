# SOFTWARE

## Before the parts arrive

| Item | Cost | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| FPV simulator | Paid, small | Stick time. The item most likely to decide whether the quad survives its first month |

## To build and configure

| Item | Cost | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller configurator | Free | Ports, modes, rates, failsafe. Where you'll spend the most time |
| Radio link configurator | Free | Flashing and binding transmitter to receiver |
| ESC configurator | Free | Motor direction and ESC settings |
| Transmitter companion software | Free | Model setup and backups |

## Supporting

| Item | Cost | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Wiring diagram tool | Free | Draw it before you solder, annotate it after |
| Propulsion calculator | Paid, small | Sanity-check thrust and current draw before ordering |
| Blackbox log viewer | Free | Tuning, once you're flying |

> On macOS the ESC configurator is browser-based and needs a Chromium browser.
> Everything else has a native Mac build.

**Also required, not a purchase:** FAA recreational registration, and a visual
