Entry 06 celebrated the soldering iron arriving ahead of its window. The
celebration did not survive first contact: the AiXun T3A is **defective out
of the box**, and it's going back to Amazon for a $135.68 refund.

The replacement is already ordered — a **YIHUA 939D+ digital station** for
**$57.23**, less than half what the AiXun cost. It's ESD-safe, has proper
digital temperature control from 392 to 896 °F, and YIHUA has been the
default recommendation for budget stations for years. If it had been in the
original shopping list, nobody would have blinked.

## What half the price actually buys

The honest trade is wattage: the AiXun was a 200W station, the YIHUA is
**75W**. For most of this build that difference is invisible — the twelve
motor-wire joints on the ESC are small pads that any temperature-controlled
iron handles.

Where it will show is the same two joints I keep writing about: the ESC's
fat battery pads and the 12 AWG XT60 lead. Wattage is recovery speed — how
fast the tip pumps heat back when a big copper mass is drinking it away. A
200W station muscles through; a 75W station needs technique in its place:

- the **largest chisel tip** in the box, never the fine conical,
- **maximum temperature** for those two joints,
- and patience — hold, let the joint truly come up to heat, then feed solder.

A budget iron with good technique beats an expensive iron with bad technique
every day of the week. I was going to have to learn the technique anyway.

## Where that leaves the build

Running total is now **$855.20**, which drops back to roughly **$720** once
the AiXun refund posts — meaning the working iron effectively saved about
$70 against the original plan. The bench state: everything is here except
the flight controller and the replacement iron. The soldering queue from
this morning's entry is unchanged, just re-parked until the YIHUA lands.

The lesson going in the log: **power the tool on the day it arrives.** The
AiXun sat in its box for nothing — the return window doesn't care how busy
the week was, and a defect found on day one is a refund instead of a fight.
