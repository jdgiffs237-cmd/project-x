I'd rather hear from you than not. If something here was useful, wrong, or
worth arguing about — especially if it was wrong — get in touch.

Things worth writing to me about:

- **A correction.** If I've made a mistake about a component, a regulation, or
  a number, tell me. Corrections get appended to the entry with credit.
- **You've built one.** Anyone who has actually flown what I'm about to attempt
  knows things a spec sheet won't tell me.
- **You're starting from zero too.** Comparing notes beats making the same
  mistake twice in two places.

Email is the reliable one — I read all of it, and I'll usually answer within a
few days. Text works too. If you call and I don't pick up, it's because I don't
answer numbers I don't recognize; leave a message or send a text and I will.

For anything technical, email is genuinely better: I can look at links, photos,
and part numbers, and the thread stays searchable when I need to find it again
six months later.
