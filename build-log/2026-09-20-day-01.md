- **First charge.** Started on `Charge` at 0.9 A with the pack loose on carpet;
  stopped, moved it into the LiPo bag on a non-flammable surface, restarted at
  1.5 A. Mode was correct from the start — on this charger `Charge` balances the
  cells at the end of the cycle; the separate `Balance` task only equalizes
  without filling the pack. Procedure written up as
  [WI-02.5](../Project%20One%20-%20Standard%20Drone/Routing/WI-02.5-first-charge-and-battery-handling.md).
- **Lesson:** the charging surface is a step in the procedure now, not a footnote.
- **Lesson:** charger mode names are not portable. iMAX-style `Balance` means
  balance-charge; HOTA-style `Charge` already balances and `Balance` is a
  standalone utility. Read the manual per charger, don't carry conventions over.
