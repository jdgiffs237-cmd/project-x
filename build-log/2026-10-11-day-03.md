## What actually happened

**First contact over USB.** Connected to Betaflight Configurator 2026.6.1. The
board arrived running **Betaflight 4.5.3** (target `SKYSTARSH7HD`, a cloud build
from Nov 25 2025).

- Warning on connect: *"the accelerometer is enabled but it is not
  calibrated."* Normal on a new board. Fix: Setup tab → **Calibrate
  Accelerometer**, board flat and still. Has to be redone once the FC is mounted
  on the frame.
- The Firmware info **Download** link for the factory build returned **404 Not
  Found**. Betaflight's build server only keeps cloud builds for a while. The
  firmware on the board was unaffected.

**Flashed Betaflight 2026.6.2** (released 16 Sep 2026) from Firmware Flasher →
Board & Build:

| Setting | Chose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Radio protocol | CRSF | ELRS receiver in Phase 2 — no reflash needed later |
| Telemetry | Automatically included | Comes with CRSF |
| OSD protocol | Digital | HD board; digital FPV in Phase 3 |
| Motor protocol | DSHOT | AM32 ESC; DShot600 gets set in WI-04 |
| Other options | Acro Trainer, GPS, LED Strip, Optical Flow, Pin IO — all kept | 2 MB flash; costs nothing until switched on |

Build was 493,346 bytes (about a quarter of the chip), served from the cache.
Accepted the "this wipes settings" warning — the board only had factory defaults.

**The flash hung.** "Please wait" for about 10 minutes, with no progress bar.
Device Manager showed the board as **DFU in FS Mode** under *Universal Serial Bus
devices*, on the WinUSB driver (libwdi, 6.1.7600.16385). So the driver was the
right one, and the board really was in DFU. Next try was flashing with **No
Reboot Sequence**, or from app.betaflight.com in Edge — *(record which one
worked)*.

**First battery power.** Battery connected to the stack. The Power & Battery tab
read **24.49 V, 6 cells detected**, so the VBAT line through the 8-pin harness
works. Amperage showed **0.68 A / 16.7 W** with nothing spinning. That's most
likely the current-sensor scale (still at 290, not yet set from the KO60II
manual), not real draw. *(confirm: smoke stopper in line for the first power-up?
continuity audit done first? anything warm?)*

**Planning.** The [roadmap](https://claude.ai/artifact/TSb7eo9mAvQgAtfM15WNNo) got:
