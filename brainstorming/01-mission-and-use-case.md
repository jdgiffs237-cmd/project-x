## Guiding questions to argue with yourself about

- If this drone crashes on flight #1 and is destroyed, how do I feel? Your answer determines your budget and whether you should build a cheap trainer first.
- Am I optimizing for the *build* or the *result*? A person who wants aerial photos of their house should probably buy a drone. A person who wants to understand drones should build one.
- What's the *simplest* aircraft that satisfies the mission? Every added capability costs weight, money, and failure modes.
- Do I actually have a legal place to fly this within a reasonable drive? Check before you spend anything.
